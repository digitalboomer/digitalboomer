## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for senior adults and users with emerging digital literacy. The aesthetic pairs the structural clarity of a functional wireframe with strict accessibility engineering: stark contrast, deliberate spatial orientation, and zero decorative noise. 

The visual style is **Functional Wireframe / High-Contrast Minimalist**. Every line, container, and visual cue serves an immediate pedagogical and navigational purpose. Rather than abstract iconography, ambiguous gestures, or subtle micro-interactions, the UI prioritizes:
- **Literal Affordances:** Actionable items look distinctly mechanical, tactile, and unmistakable.
- **Cognitive Calm:** High baseline whitespace, strict left-aligned reading paths, and complete elimination of distracting motion or decorative ornaments.
- **Constant Orientation:** Clear persistent context (breadcrumbs, persistent step trackers, explicit screen names) so the user never feels lost.
